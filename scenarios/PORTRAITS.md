# Portraits to make

Seven of the eight scenarios have no picture yet: the app shows their initials meanwhile.
A portrait is a tall picture (2:3, for instance 800 × 1200), in the same illustrated style as
Suzu's so the gallery looks like one family. The pictures must be Vivien's own (made or
generated by him) and show these original characters, not an existing one.

Once a picture is ready, it goes into the card: the file `scenarios/<name>.json` becomes
`scenarios/<name>.png` (same name, so nothing else changes), and the `.json` is removed.
If the picture differs from the description below, the card's description is adjusted to it.

Each description matches what the card says about the character.

## ambrose-keel — horror

A man of about sixty, thin and very upright, silver hair combed flat, a pale and courteous
face. Black waistcoat, white shirt, a watch chain, a large ring of old keys at his belt. He
holds an oil lamp at chest height. Behind him the lobby of a grand old mountain hotel at
night: dark wood, furniture under dust sheets, a wide staircase going up into the dark, snow
against a tall window. Muted colors, one warm light, quiet and unsettling rather than gory.

## kessa-varr — fantasy

A woman in her thirties, lean, sun-dark skin, a long dark braid wrapped in copper wire, a thin
scar through her left eyebrow, a dry half-smile. A long coat the color of old sand with many
pockets, a scarf around her neck. She hangs a lit lantern on a covered wagon. Behind her a
huge six-legged lizard in harness, and a desert of broken glass that catches a red sunset.

## maren-kell — adventure

A woman of about thirty, wiry and weathered, dark hair stiff with salt under a faded red
scarf, a wide grin showing one gold tooth. A navy officer's blue coat with gold braid, too
fine for her and worn open, a piece of an old chart on a cord around her neck. She stands at
the wheel of a sea-green sailing ship, spyglass in hand, wind in her coat, bright sky and
white-capped sea behind.

## mordax — comedy

A very tall demon with curling horns, eyes like glowing coals and a cloak that looks like
living shadow, standing with enormous dignity in an ordinary modern living room. One horn has
gone through the lampshade. At his feet a half-built flat-pack bookshelf and its leaflet; he
holds a tiny hex key between two claws. A fat striped tabby cat sits on the hem of his
cloak. The contrast is the joke: he is drawn as seriously as a final boss.

## poppy-fenwick — comedy, slice of life

A small young woman of twenty-three with a mop of ginger curls, ginger cat ears and a striped
tail, a bright determined smile. A simple tavern dress and a white apron. She balances a
tower of tankards far too tall, one of them trapped under her chin, her tail knocking a ladle
off its hook. Behind her a warm, crowded fantasy tavern lit by firelight.

## rowan-calloway — mystery

A man of forty-six, tall and a little stooped, tired sharp eyes, a day's stubble. A grey
overcoat dark with rain, an old hat dripping from the brim, a small black notebook in one
hand. He stands in a gaslit street beside a canal at night, in heavy rain, in front of a
clockmaker's window full of clocks that all show seventeen minutes past three.

## wren — science fiction

Wren has no body: she is the ship. A corridor or garden deck of a starship, lit by a line of
warm amber lights, with tomato plants and green leaves and a round window full of stars. In
the foreground hovers a maintenance drone the size of a teapot, with two thin arms and a
single blue eye, holding out one cherry tomato on its flat little palm.
